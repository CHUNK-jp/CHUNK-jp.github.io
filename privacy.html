Privacy Policy

This policy applies to all CHUNK-jp tools and browser utilities: Attic, MirrorHealth, Daily Pulse, AgentWatch, Loan Calculator, BMI Calculator, and the Text Encryption Tool.

Last updated: June 25, 2026
TL;DR — The short version

We don't collect your data. The tools don't send anything anywhere.

All computation runs locally on your device — no server receives your data
No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking pixels, no analytics SDK
Third-party services (hosting, optional ads) are disclosed below in full
01

What Our Tools Collect

The CHUNK-jp tools process all data exclusively on your device. At no point does any tool transmit your files, health records, search queries, news reading habits, AI agent logs, financial inputs, or body measurements to an external server.

Attic
Builds a file index entirely within macOS sandbox storage. No filenames or paths leave the app.
MirrorHealth
Reads Apple Health exports only from your local disk. AI analysis runs via Ollama on your machine. Health data never leaves your device.
Daily Pulse
Fetches news from public RSS feeds directly from your device. Summaries are generated locally. Your reading history is stored only in local app data.
AgentWatch
Monitors AI agent activity through a local CLI process. All telemetry data stays on-device and is never reported externally.
Browser Utilities Loan · BMI · Text Encryption
All calculations run in-browser. No input data is submitted to any server. Encrypted text exists only in your browser session and is never stored remotely.

Where ads are shown on this portal, Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to serve contextual or personalized advertising based on your browsing activity. Google acts as an independent data controller for this. You can manage your ad personalization preferences at adssettings.google.com.

No Analytics
We run no analytics platform of our own

CHUNK-jp does not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Plausible, or any other analytics service. There is no tracking code embedded by CHUNK-jp on any page or in any tool.

Cookies & Local Storage

CHUNK-jp sets no cookies of its own. The browser utilities may use localStorage solely to preserve your in-session state (e.g. calculation inputs) between page refreshes. This data never leaves your browser.

Data We Never Collect

To be explicit, none of the following is ever collected, stored, or transmitted by CHUNK-jp or its tools:

Health or biometric data

Device identifiers or fingerprints

Your Rights

Because CHUNK-jp does not collect personal data, most data subject rights (access, deletion, portability) are satisfied by the nature of the architecture itself — there is no data to request or delete. The following applies to any data held by third-party services listed above:

Right of access
Contact the relevant third-party service (GitHub, Google) directly using the links in Section 02.
Right to deletion
For browser utility localStorage, clear site data in your browser settings at any time.
Ad personalization
Manage or opt out of Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
Questions
Open a GitHub issue at github.com/CHUNK-jp and we will respond promptly.
07

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes — such as the introduction of any data collection — will be announced in the relevant GitHub repository. We have no mechanism to notify you proactively, which is intentional: collecting email addresses to send policy updates would itself be a privacy concern.

Questions or concerns?

If you have questions about this privacy policy or any aspect of how your data is handled, please open an issue on GitHub or reach out via our contact page. All questions are welcomed and will be answered publicly so the answers benefit everyone.
